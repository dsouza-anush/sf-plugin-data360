# Code of conduct

We expect project participation to be respectful, constructive, and safe. Treat people with empathy; critique ideas and code rather than individuals; avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and disclosure of another person's private information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior is unsafe or disruptive. Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer route. Do not place sensitive personal, security, customer, org, or credential information in a public issue.

Maintainers should assess reports promptly, limit access to those handling the report, avoid retaliation, and communicate outcomes to the extent privacy permits. If no verified private contact is published yet, request a restricted contact route without including incident details.
